SIGNAL OF THE DAY

This is a tactical signal, not a structural reversal. The IRGC closed the strait specifically in response to Trump maintaining the US port blockade after the opening announcement, framing it as a "breach of trust." The rapid open-and-close sequence is the most analytically important signal of the week because it reveals the negotiating mechanic Iran is running: offer something, demand reciprocation, withdraw when reciprocation does not come, reapply pressure. This is leverage management, not chaos.
