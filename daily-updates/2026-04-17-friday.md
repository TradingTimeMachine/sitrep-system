MODEL IMPACT: COMPLICATED
This is not a falsification event but it is the most significant single-day movement against the Issue 001 baseline since it was set. Three things happened simultaneously today that interact in analytically important ways.
First, Iran's Hormuz declaration is a tactical concession, not a strategic one. The opening is explicitly tied to the ceasefire duration -- it is conditional and revocable. The mechanism that made Hormuz a structural veto for the IRGC (the mine problem, the physical closure) has not been resolved -- it has been voluntarily suspended. This is a different thing. The IRGC gave something it can take back. That is leverage management, not capitulation.
Second, the Israel-Lebanon 10-day ceasefire removes the most acute daily escalation pressure from the system. The truce includes Hezbollah in the cessation of hostilities, was announced by Trump, and came into effect Thursday night -- though Lebanese military reported Israeli ceasefire violations within hours of it taking effect. This changes the pressure calculus on Iran -- one front has cooled, reducing the multi-front stress that was accelerating the IRGC's intransigence.
